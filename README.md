# Clicker Game (My first Godot Game)

In this game, you just need to click on the button on the center of the sceen to earn points... as you get points, you can buy upgrades to earn even more Points, all of that to do a rebirth and start all over again!

Honestly, I dont know much of Godot, but I tried to make a simple game in that Engine just to experience it!

## What's the objective?
Try to get as many rebirth, upgrades and clicks as you can!
